Licence compliance checks

Members of my Commercial Plan may sell physical prints made from my models. To confirm that the licence terms are being followed, I may ask a licensee for information where there is reasonable cause.

What I may ask for

  • your name or company name and contact details;
  • the addresses of your listings, shops or points of sale;
  • confirmation that your subscription is active;
  • summary revenue figures for products made from my models;
  • photographs of products or listings, to check attribution.

I ask only for what is needed to answer the specific question and I do not require you to disclose confidential business information beyond that.

Legal basis. Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, because the check forms part of performing the licence agreement, and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, because I have a legitimate interest in enforcing the terms under which my work is used.

How long I keep it

Information collected for a compliance check is deleted once the matter is closed, and at the latest [12] months afterwards. Where a dispute is unresolved, I keep it until that dispute is settled. Statutory retention periods under German commercial and tax law remain unaffected.

My Discord server

I run a Discord server for members and followers. If you join it, I can see your Discord username, your profile picture, the roles assigned to you, and anything you post in the channels.

Where you link your Thangs membership to Discord, roles are assigned automatically according to your plan and removed again when the membership ends.

Legal basis. Article 6(1)(f) GDPR — my legitimate interest in running a community around my designs and in giving members the access they subscribed for.

Discord Netherlands B.V. and Discord Inc. operate the platform and process your data under their own privacy policy, including transfers to the United States. Joining the server is voluntary, and you can leave at any time. Content you have posted stays visible unless you delete it or ask me to.
